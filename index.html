GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

How do I book a cleaning? ＋

Choose your package, fill in the booking form and send your request. Our team will contact you to confirm availability.

Are wall cleaning charges included? ＋

Wall cleaning is an optional package choice. Select the option in the booking form to see the guide price.

Is my booking confirmed when I submit the form? ＋

No. Submitting a request is not a confirmed appointment. Our team must confirm the date, time and service scope.

Which areas do you serve? ＋

Contact us with your Pune-area location to confirm whether service is available at your address.
